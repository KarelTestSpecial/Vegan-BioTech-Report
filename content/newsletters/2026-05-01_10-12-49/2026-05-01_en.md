# Top Stories This Week  

* **Upside Foods** (Impact: 9) – 15 Mar 2026 – Upside Foods has earned FDA clearance for its cultivated chicken, marking the first approved lab‑grown meat product. [Link](https://www.reuters.com/food/upside-foods-gets-fda-approval-for-cultivated-chicken-2026-03-15/) This approval heralds a new era for sustainable meat, and investors should monitor regulatory pathways for other cultured meat categories.

[Link](https://www.foodnavigator.com/news/2026/04/10/perfect-day-raises-150m-precision-fermentation) The funding underscores strong investor confidence in precision‑fermented dairy, suggesting further expansion into other plant‑based milk categories.

* **Novozymes** (Impact: 8) – 12 Apr 2026 – Novozymes and DSM have teamed up to create precision‑fermented whey, expanding the portfolio of vegan dairy ingredients. [Link](https://www.foodnavigator.com/news/2026/04/12/novozymes-dsm-whey-fermentation) The joint venture signals consolidation in the precision‑fermentation space, indicating broader adoption of vegan protein sources.  

* **Eat Just** (Impact: 8) – 30 Mar 2026 – Eat Just has launched gas‑fermentation cultured egg whites, providing a cruelty‑free substitute for traditional chicken eggs. [Link](https://www.reuters.com/food/eat-just-launches-gas-fermentation-egg-whites-2026-03-30/) Eat Just's entry into cultured egg whites diversifies the alternative protein landscape, with potential ripple effects for egg‑dependent industries.  

* **Geltor** (Impact: 7) – 10 Apr 2026 – Geltor has joined forces with Adidas to develop fermented‑collagen vegan leather, targeting broader adoption in the fashion industry. [Link](https://www.businessinsider.com/geltor-adidas-vegan-leather-2026-04-10) The Geltor‑Adidas partnership demonstrates that fermented‑collagen leather is moving toward mainstream apparel, prompting watchful eyes on scaling efforts.  

* **Bolt Threads** (Impact: 7) – 28 Apr 2026 – Bolt Threads has unveiled a spider‑silk fabric designed for automotive interiors, offering high‑strength, cruelty‑free material. [Link](https://www.wired.com/story/bolt-threads-spider-silk-automotive-2026-04-28/) Bolt Threads' spider‑silk fabric for cars showcases high‑performance vegan materials, suggesting future applications in other automotive components.  

* **Ecovative** (Impact: 7) – 01 May 2026 – Ecovative is expanding its mycelium‑based packaging solutions to substitute plastic foam in electronic products, cutting waste and carbon emissions. [Link](https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-ecovative-mushroom-packaging-2026-05-01) Ecovative's push to replace plastic foam with mycelium packaging aligns with tightening environmental regulations, making it a key area to follow.
