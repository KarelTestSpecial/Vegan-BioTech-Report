# Vegan BioTech Report – Edition 01 Feb 2026

The vegan biotech industry has seen significant advancements in recent weeks, with companies like MycoWorks and Bolt Threads making strides in sustainable materials, while Quorn and Perfect Day expand their presence in the food sector.
